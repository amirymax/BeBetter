# **BeBetter - Stronger. Smarter. Better**

BeBetter is an engaging platform designed to help users unlock their full potential through structured levels and stages. The project combines mental and physical exercises to promote consistent self-improvement and personal growth.

## **Features**
- 🧠 **Personalized Tasks:** Levels tailored to individual preferences, including mental challenges like reading and quizzes, and physical tasks like push-ups and running.
- 📊 **Progress Tracking:** Users can view their achievements, current levels, and completed tasks on a dynamic dashboard.
- 🤖 **Seamless Integration:** A Telegram bot verifies task completion, including AI-based video analysis for physical exercises.
- 💡 **Motivational Design:** Each level is enhanced with inspiring quotes and resources, making the journey rewarding and fun.
